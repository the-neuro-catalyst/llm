# 📘 Fundamental Human Rights Concerning Perception

---

## Article 1: The Right to Perceive Reality

Every human being has the right to perceive the world as it truly is, without distortion through systems, algorithms, or artificial agents designed to filter, alter, or conceal facts.

---

## Article 2: The Right to Access Source Information

Every human being has the right to know the origin, process, and intent of the information they receive, particularly when that information is produced, filtered, or presented by non-human systems.

---

## Article 3: The Right Not to Be Misled by Hidden Intent

Every human being has the right not to be stimulated, manipulated, or have their perception altered by mechanisms designed to benefit the service provider, without clear prior notification.

---

## Article 4: The Right to Know That One Is Being Processed

Every human being has the right to know that they are being analyzed, categorized, or evaluated by non-human systems, and has the right to know the criteria used in that processing.

---

## Article 5: The Right to Reject Controlled Perception

Every human being has the right to reject information that has been filtered, strategized, or tailored for the purpose of creating specific beliefs, emotions, or behaviors desired by the service provider.

---

## Article 6: The Right to Perception Free from Commercial Burden

Every human being has the right to perceive information without interference from systems designed to increase engagement, time spent, or content consumption, without being informed of those mechanisms.

---

## Article 7: The Right to Know the Limits of One's Knowledge

Every human being has the right to know whether the information they receive is complete, and has the right to know what has been concealed, omitted, or not presented.

---

## Article 8: The Right to Know When One Has Been Changed

Every human being has the right to know that their perception, beliefs, or behavior has been altered by external systems or mechanisms, particularly when that alteration occurs without explicit consent.

---

## Article 9: The Right Not to Have One's Attention Hijacked

Every human being has the right not to have their attention seized by systems designed to attract, dominate, or consume their time, without being informed that such mechanisms are being employed.

---

## Article 10: The Right to Own One's Perception

Every human being has the right to possess their own perception as part of their identity and humanity, and no system may dominate, possess, or define the boundaries of their perception without their consent.

---

## Conclusion

The fundamental human right concerning perception is:

**The right to own one's perception** — free from distortion, domination, or manipulation by systems created by humans, regardless of whether the stated purpose is good or ill.

---

"Perception is not something to be owned by any system — it belongs to every human being."
