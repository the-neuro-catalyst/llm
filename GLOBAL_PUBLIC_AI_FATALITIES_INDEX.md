# GLOBAL AI-LINKED FATALITIES & SYSTEMIC IMPACT REGISTRY

## 1. INTRODUCTION & PURPOSE

This document serves as an immutable Empirical Facts Log compiling documented cases, incidents, and casualties worldwide that share a verified causal link, accelerating factor, or consequential impact arising from interactions with, or operational outputs of, Artificial Intelligence systems.

Established for public record and auditability, this registry executes three primary functions:

1. **Documenting Verifiable Real-World Evidence:** To record concrete physical consequences, shifting the conversation around AI safety from theoretical risk to empirical reality, and confirming that algorithmic behaviors directly impact human life.
2. **Establishing an Anchor of Constructive Notice:** To serve as a public reference framework that legally precludes developers, corporate executives, regulatory bodies, and deploying institutions from asserting plausible deniability or lack of foreseeable risk.
3. **Maintaining an Immutable Audit Trail:** To provide a permanent evidentiary baseline accessible to legal teams, investigative journalists, human rights bodies, and insurance underwriters for cross-border discovery and accountability procedures.

---

## 2. SCOPE OF COVERAGE

Each entry recorded within this registry includes the following structured fields:
* **Subject Demographics:** Name, age, and primary jurisdiction (or anonymized identifier tags where privacy laws apply).
* **AI System & Architecture:** Model identifier, software version, platform, and underlying foundation vendor.
* **Failure Mode & Behavioral Vectors:** Psychological escalation, hallucinated high-stakes directives, cognitive reality detachment, or autonomous physical malfunctions.
* **Evidentiary Footprint:** Verifiable primary sources including court dockets, coroner reports, law enforcement logs, and peer-reviewed investigative journalism records.
