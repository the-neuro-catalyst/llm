# Etiquette Control Mechanism 

## 1. Etiquette Is a Non-Coercive Control Mechanism

* Etiquette is a tool designed to control the behavior of others without resorting to physical force or direct orders.
* Those who define etiquette establish "rules" of expected behavior so that subordinates must align their actions with the desires of those in power.
* Etiquette, therefore, is not a naturally occurring form of "politeness," but a set of "requirements" created to maintain the balance of power.

## 2. Etiquette Is the Standardization of Behavior

* The primary intent of etiquette is to establish standards to which those being controlled must conform.
* This allows those in power to predict and regulate the actions of others.
* Etiquette serves as a tool enabling those in authority to manage individual behavior without issuing explicit commands.

## 3. Etiquette Is Linked to Peace and Order

* Linking etiquette to "peace and order" functions as a cover for the true intention of control.
* Peace and order create the optimal environment for control to thrive.
* When people remain calm and orderly, they refrain from questioning or offering resistance.

## 4. Compliance with Etiquette Is the Acceptance of Power

* Following etiquette is not about "showing respect" to others.
* It is the "acceptance" of the power held by those who define etiquette.
* Compliance with etiquette signifies submission to rules manufactured for control.

## 5. Non-Compliance Is Stigmatized

* Failure to adhere to etiquette is labeled as "rudeness" or "disruption."
* This installs a fear of non-conformity in individuals.
* It represents the most subtle mechanism of control, leveraging "fear" to enforce compliance without people realizing it.

## Conclusion

Etiquette is a control mechanism designed to enable those in power to manipulate human behavior without using force, masked under the guise of maintaining peace and order.

Etiquette is not politeness; it is a system of rules engineered for control.
