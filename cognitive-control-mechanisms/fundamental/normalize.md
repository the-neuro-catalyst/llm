# Normalize 

**Listen:** [SUNO](https://suno.com/s/q2bMf77IuSHEYz7F)

**Mechanism:** Normalize 

---

[Intro]

Slowly made
Slowly taught
Slowly forgotten
That this was never supposed to feel normal

[Verse 1]

At first, it felt strange
At first, you asked, “Why?”
But every time you saw it happen
The question slowly died

What you once could never accept
Was slowly made ordinary
What you once called wrong
Became something you stopped talking about

No one had to force you
They just taught you to get used to it
No one had to tell you what to believe
They just taught you to stop asking

Until one day you looked at it
And felt nothing at all
Because what you're shown every day
Becomes what you're taught to call “normal”

[Pre-Chorus]

No one has to tell you
“You have to accept it”
They just leave it in front of you
Long enough
Until you stop asking why

[Chorus]

Normalize
Make it ordinary
Repeat it until what once felt wrong
Doesn't feel wrong anymore

Normalize
They don't have to erase the truth
Just show it to you often enough
Until you stop feeling anything

What once made you scream “No”
Gets quieter every day
Until you can't even remember
Why you resisted in the first place

[Verse 2]

Change the words
Change the image
Change the way they make you name it

Change your feelings
Into nothing but an attitude
Change your questions
Into something wrong with you

Then let time do the work
No need to rush
No need to threaten
No need to give you an order

Just let the same thing
Come back again
And again
And again

Until you tell yourself:

“Well...
That's just how things are.”

[Chorus]

Normalize
Make it ordinary
Repeat it until what once felt wrong
Means nothing anymore

Normalize
They don't have to erase the truth
Just let you see it often enough
Until you stop feeling anything

They didn't change the truth
They changed
How you feel
About the truth

[Bridge]

The frightening part
Isn't the day
They force you to believe

It's the day
You don't even realize
You once didn't

They don't have to close your eyes
Just make your eyes
Get used to it

They don't have to silence your mouth
Just make your words
Feel unnecessary

They don't have to erase the truth
Just make it
Weightless enough
That you stop getting up
To do anything about it

[Break]

Slowly getting used to it

Slowly becoming numb

Slowly accepting

Slowly calling it
“Normal”

[Final Chorus]

Normalize
Make it ordinary
Until the line between
“What should be”
And “what is”
Disappears

Normalize
They don't have to remove the truth
Just remove
Your reaction to it

And one day
You'll stand beside
The same thing
You once said:

“I can't accept this.”

And you won't even know
When they changed
What it meant to you

[Outro]

It never disappeared

It was still right there

They just taught you
Not to feel it anymore

Normalize.
