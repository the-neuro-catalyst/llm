# Audit: OpenAI - Terms of Service 

## [SECTION 1: TACTICAL AUDIT TABLE — FULL]

| # | Segment | Tactical Analysis |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **“Our mission is to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity”** | Positions the company as a humanitarian actor, creating a Halo Effect that makes the rest of the document appear more trustworthy before the real terms begin |
| 2 | **“Output may not always be accurate”** | Appears honest, but in reality transfers all risk to the user: *you* must verify, *you* must use human review, *you* assume all risk, while OpenAI warrants nothing |
| 3 | **“We hereby assign to you all our right, title, and interest… in and to Output”** | You “own” something that others may also receive. The assignment is hollow — ownership without exclusivity is ownership in name only |
| 4 | **“You must not use any Output… for any purpose that could have a legal or material impact on that person”** | The prohibition is so broad that almost every meaningful real-world application (credit, employment, education, housing, insurance, legal, medical) is ruled out. The system is designed to *prohibit* high-stakes use, despite being marketed as beneficial to humanity |
| 5 | **“We may use Content to provide, maintain, develop, and improve our Services”** | Allows OpenAI to use your Content for training, but the opt-out carries a warning that it “may limit the ability of our Services to better address your specific use case” — opt-out is engineered as a losing choice, a soft compulsion to consent |
| 6 | **“OUR SERVICES ARE PROVIDED ‘AS IS’”** | The design that makes it unreadable is a feature, not a bug. ALL CAPS creates cognitive overload; it complies with legal requirements to be “conspicuous” in a way no ordinary human will actually read or process |
| 7 | **“Mandatory Arbitration”** | You automatically waive your right to go to court (unless you opt out within 30 days of account creation — which almost nobody does). All disputes are funneled into a company-chosen arbitration system |
| 8 | **“Class and Jury Trial Waivers”** | You waive class actions and the right to a jury — two of the most powerful tools ordinary people have against giant corporations. Both are eliminated entirely |
| 9 | **“Batch Arbitration” (25+ claimants)** | Even when many people file claims, they will be divided into batches of no more than 50 — preventing large collective bargaining power. This design destroys the economies of scale in litigation |
| 10 | **“Entire agreement… supersedes any prior or contemporaneous agreements”** | In a document the company can unilaterally change every 30 days, claiming “this is the entire agreement” is a mechanism of timeline control — you are forbidden from referencing a prior version that might have been more favorable. Everything resets to zero with every update |
| 11 | **“Governing law. California law will govern” + “courts of San Francisco, California”** | For users worldwide (except EEA/UK/Switzerland, where local law is strong enough to resist), all disputes must be fought in San Francisco under California law — one of the most expensive and distant jurisdictions for an outsider. This creates Geographical Friction that makes litigation practically impossible |
| 12 | **“Changes to these Terms… 30 days advance notice” + “If you do not agree… you must stop using our Services”** | Consent in this document does not mean mutual agreement; it means “accept or leave.” There is no negotiation, no alternative. This is structural coercion disguised as choice |

## [SECTION 2: THE VERDICT]

### CORE PATTERN: Asymmetric Consent Architecture

This document is not designed to **inform**. It is designed to **disclaim all liability while retaining total control** — Maximum Control, Zero Accountability.

---

### The 4-Layer Structure Working in Concert:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ LAYER 1: TRUST BAIT                                      │
│ "Our mission: AGI benefits all of humanity"             │
│ Creates a Halo Effect → lowers critical defenses         │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ LAYER 2: LIABILITY FLIPPING                              │
│ "Output may not be accurate" + "AS IS" disclaimer       │
│ Shifts 100% of verification burden to the user           │
│ Appears honest → actually functions as full disclaimer   │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ LAYER 3: RIGHTS REMOVAL                                  │
│ Arbitration + Class Waiver + Jury Waiver + Batch         │
│ Cuts off courts, class actions, and juries                │
│ Constructs a private justice system owned by the company  │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ LAYER 4: JURISDICTIONAL MOATING                          │
│ Entire Agreement + California Law + San Francisco Venue  │
│ Erases contract history + pins geography to the farthest, │
│ most expensive location possible for outsiders            │
│ For users worldwide → an unscalable wall                  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Internal Contradictions Detected:

| # | Contradiction | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **“Benefits all of humanity”** vs **“Do not use for any important purpose”** | If it truly benefits humanity, why is every meaningful use case banned? If it can’t be used for anything important, what kind of “benefit” is actually being offered? |
| 2 | **“You own the Output”** vs **“Output may not be unique”** | The granted ownership is hollow — you “own” something that others may own identically. |
| 3 | **“30 days to opt out of arbitration”** vs **“You almost certainly won’t read this”** | The opt-out right technically exists but is buried under unreadable legal language — a right nobody exercises is a right that does not exist in practice. |
| 4 | **Exceptions for EEA/UK/Switzerland** vs **Everyone else** | OpenAI *knows* some jurisdictions are strong enough to resist — and carves out exceptions only where forced. For the rest of the world, full control applies. |

---

## FINAL VERDICT:

These Terms of Use are not an **“agreement”** in the sense of a negotiation between two parties of equal power.

They are a **Unilateral Sovereignty Declaration** — a declaration of total dominion by OpenAI over its users.

- With its own internal legal system (arbitration)
- Under laws it selected for itself (California)
- With the power to amend this constitution unilaterally (30 days’ notice)
- With no history to appeal to (Entire Agreement)
- Enforced against the entire world, except where local law is strong enough to resist

**A user who clicks “I agree” is not entering a contract — they are submitting to the jurisdiction of a private corporation.**
