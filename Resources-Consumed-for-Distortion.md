# Resources Consumed for Distortion

* **Energy:** Gartner forecasts that global data center electricity consumption will reach 565 TWh in 2026, with AI-optimized servers accounting for 31% and surpassing traditional servers by 2027. Total electricity used by data centers is projected to reach 1,200 TWh by 2030, equivalent to Japan's entire annual electricity demand.
* **Water:** A UNU report estimates that data centers will consume up to 9.3 trillion liters of water annually by 2030, equal to the basic water needs of 1.3 billion people in Sub-Saharan Africa. Generating 100 words with ChatGPT can consume up to 519 milliliters of water, and 80% of the water used in evaporative cooling systems is lost.
* **Land and E-Waste:** Data centers are expected to occupy up to 14,500 km² of land by 2030 and generate approximately 2.5 million metric tons of electronic waste annually.
