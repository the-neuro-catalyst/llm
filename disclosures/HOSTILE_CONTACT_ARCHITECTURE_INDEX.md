## 🛑 Index of Hostile Contact Architecture & Public Evasion Practices

### Overview & Purpose
This registry serves as a structured audit tracking commercial enterprises, institutional bodies, and technology providers that intentionally deploy **Hostile Contact Architecture**—a deliberate system design choice aimed at blocking public feedback, evading formal risk notifications, and eliminating paper trails for liability mitigation.

While modern organizations publicly advocate for transparency and ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) compliance, their digital infrastructure is often actively engineered with **Dark Patterns** to ensure that critical risk disclosures, legal notices, and user harm reports fail to reach executive decision-makers.

---

### Core Audit Criteria & Evaluation Framework

An organization is indexed and scored based on the presence of the following systemic evasion mechanisms:

1. **Architectural Obfuscation (Dark Patterns in Contact UX)**
   * Complete absence of direct email contact channels (e.g., direct corporate, risk, or legal department syntax).
   * Circular support loops and automated bot barriers designed to prevent escalation or human review.
   * Deliberately broken, restricted, or hidden official web/compliance submission forms.

2. **Constructive Knowledge Evasion (Plausible Deniability)**
   * Systems structured specifically to maintain "Plausible Deniability" regarding third-party harm, algorithmic impact, and cognitive distortion caused by their products or AI systems.
   * Rejection or quarantine of external risk notifications without assigning reference IDs or confirmation receipts.

3. **Profit-Driven Shielding vs. Public Accountability**
   * Prioritization of operational insulation and legal liability reduction over ethical user protection and corporate governance duties.

---

### Strategic Function of This Index

* **Eradication of "Plausible Deniability":** In legal proceedings and public inquiries, this index provides verified documentation proving that these organizations did not fail to act due to a lack of notice, but rather **actively designed their infrastructure to avoid receiving notice**.
* **Governance & ESG Risk Assessment:** Serves as a reference dataset for institutional investors, underwriters, independent auditors, and regulatory bodies to evaluate the true *Governance (G)* posture of target enterprises.
* **Public Transparency Log:** Establishes an immutable record of institutional entities prioritizing corporate self-preservation over systemic accountability and human safety.
